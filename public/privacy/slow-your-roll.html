Slow Your Roll Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Slow Your Roll is a separate focus and break timer app for supported Apple devices. This policy describes how the app handles information, including its optional Workspace browser. This page is hosted on the When Healing Doesn't Come website for convenience; the two apps are separate.

Timer preferences and activity

The app stores settings such as focus and break durations, schedules, break preferences, and completed-break counts on your device. These settings support the timer and its features.

Photographs you choose

When you choose photographs for break backgrounds, Apple's Photos picker lets you select images without granting the app unrestricted access to your photo library. Selected photographs are copied into the app's private local storage. Slow Your Roll does not intentionally upload or share those imported photographs with a developer-operated server or automatically synchronize them between devices. You can remove imported photographs in the app.

Optional Workspace Mode

On iPhone and iPad, Workspace Mode lets you open websites within Slow Your Roll while using the timer. The browser uses Apple's WebKit technology. The app is designed without scripts or message handlers intended to capture website passwords, form entries, or page contents for the developer.

Workspace Mode is configured to use nonpersistent website data storage, rather than intentionally retaining website cookies or other website data between browser sessions. It restricts navigation to HTTPS websites. These safeguards reduce some privacy and security risks, but do not guarantee that websites are safe or that web content is technically inaccessible to the app.

Websites you visit may independently collect information you enter, including account details, passwords, payment information, and usage data, according to their own policies. HTTPS encrypts traffic in transit but does not prevent a website from collecting information you provide to it. For sensitive accounts, consider opening the website directly in Safari.

If Workspace Mode offers saved addresses or favorites, those addresses may be stored locally to provide that feature. Slow Your Roll does not intentionally send browsing history, website passwords, or form entries to a developer-operated server. Clearing or removing app data may remove locally saved Workspace preferences.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you select synchronized breaks, Slow Your Roll uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage to exchange limited timer session information and preferences between your devices signed into the same Apple Account. Synchronization is optional and may be delayed. Imported photographs and Workspace website sessions are not intentionally synchronized by this feature. Apple's handling of iCloud information is subject to its own privacy practices.

Screen Time app blocking

On supported iPhone and iPad devices, optional app blocking uses Apple's Screen Time authorization and privacy-preserving selection tokens. Selected app, category, or website tokens may be kept in local shared app storage so the app's extension can apply restrictions. You control whether to grant authorization and use blocking.

Notifications and Live Activities

With your permission, the app can display local timer notifications and Live Activities. You can manage these features in your device settings.

Advertising, analytics, and service providers

The current app implementation is not designed to use advertising or third-party analytics, or to transmit timer history, imported photographs, Screen Time selections, or Workspace sign-in information to a developer-operated server. Apple services used for device features operate under Apple's own privacy practices. Websites opened in Workspace Mode are independent third parties.

Retention and your choices

You can adjust timer settings, disable optional synchronization and app blocking, and remove imported photographs. Uninstalling the app generally removes local app data, subject to device backups and operating-system behavior. Information synchronized through iCloud may remain in your Apple Account or on other devices.

Children's privacy

Slow Your Roll is not designed to collect children's personal information through a developer-operated service. Apple's platform rules govern Screen Time permissions.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features or data-handling practices change. The effective date above indicates the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the developer support contact listed on the Slow Your Roll App Store page.
