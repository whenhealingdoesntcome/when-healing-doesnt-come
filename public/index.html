Our Heart for Missions

Manna Church is involved in outreach and missions efforts all over the world. As a church family, we serve locally through outreaches and ministries, nationally through planting churches all over the country, and internationally through sending short-term teams overseas and supporting international missions work all over the world.

Why We Pray

Prayer is one of the weapons that God has given us to help push back the forces of darkness and to see His Kingdom advance in power. Prayer is the foundation of every mission and outreach endeavor we embark upon. It makes more of a difference than we'll ever truly know this side of heaven.
