Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

When Healing Doesn't Come: Hope for the Journey (WHDC) is a free devotional website and app. This policy explains how information is handled when you use WHDC.

Information you choose to provide

You can read devotionals and Scripture without creating an account. If you create an optional reader account, we use your email address to send sign-in codes and to manage your account. If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include in your message. Guest writers and administrators may provide account and editorial information needed to manage their work.

Reading data and device storage

WHDC uses browser or app storage to remember preferences and features such as saved items, reading progress, and other reading tools. Some features, including private journal entries, may be stored only on your device. Device-only information may be lost if you clear app or browser data or uninstall the app. If you sign in and use cloud synchronization, selected account and reading data may be stored on our servers so it can be restored or synchronized across devices. Do not assume every device-local item is included in cloud backup.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, WHDC may store a push subscription identifier, technical delivery information, your selected reminder settings, and time zone to send requested reminders or announcements. You can turn off notifications through the app or your device/browser settings.

Technical information

Our hosting and security providers may process technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as IP addresses, request details, browser information, and diagnostic logs. We use this information to deliver WHDC, troubleshoot errors, and protect the service.

Service providers

WHDC uses Cloudflare for hosting, storage, database services, and related infrastructure. Email sign-in codes are sent using Resend. WHDC also uses Google Gemini for generating narration of devotional and Scripture content. When these services process information on our behalf, their own security and data-handling practices may apply. Public Scripture and devotional text may be sent to the narration provider for audio generation; private journal entries are not part of that narration workflow.

How we use information

We use information to provide the devotional experience, maintain optional accounts, synchronize eligible data, send requested notifications and sign-in emails, respond to messages, and operate and secure WHDC. We do not sell personal information to advertisers.

Retention and deletion

Account information and cloud-synchronized data are retained as needed to provide the account until deletion or other applicable retention limits. You can request account deletion through Settings → My Account → Delete My Account. This deletes the reader account and associated cloud-saved data handled by that feature. Information stored only on your device is not automatically deleted when you delete your account; clear local app/browser data separately if desired. Certain limited technical records may remain temporarily where necessary for security, operations, or legal compliance.

Your choices

You may use WHDC without an account, choose whether to synchronize, change notification permissions, clear local data through your device or browser, and delete your reader account. For questions about your information or privacy requests, contact us using the Contact section of WHDC.

Children's privacy

WHDC is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can review the request.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features or practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we publish a revision.
