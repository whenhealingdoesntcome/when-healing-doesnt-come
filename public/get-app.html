Help me add this app ↓

iPhone or iPad

Use Safari (blue compass) or Chrome (red, yellow, green, and blue circle). You only need to do this once.

  1. Open this page in Safari or Chrome. If you are reading it inside Facebook, Instagram, or a message, use that app's menu to open the page in Safari or Chrome.
  2. Find the Share button. Look for a square with an arrow pointing up:
    Safari: Look at the bottom of the screen, or tap the page/menu button first.
    Chrome: Look near the address bar or tap the three dots (⋯) and choose Share.
  3. Choose “Add to Home Screen.” You may need to scroll down through the options.
  4. Tap “Add.” Look for the WHDC app icon on your phone's Home Screen.
Can't find the Share button?

Try scrolling to the top of the webpage so your browser's buttons appear. In Chrome, tap the three-dot menu and look for Share. If you still can't find “Add to Home Screen,” open this page in Safari and try again.

If the website opened inside Facebook, Instagram, or another app, use its menu to choose Open in browser, Open in Safari, or Open in Chrome. If that option is missing, copy the link and paste it into Safari or Chrome.

Once the Share menu opens, scroll down to find Add to Home Screen. If it is missing in Chrome, try Safari.

Android phone

Chrome is usually the easiest way to add WHDC to your Home Screen.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) near the top-right of Chrome.
  3. Tap “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.”
  4. Follow the prompts to finish. Look for the WHDC icon on your Home Screen.
Can't find the option?

Make sure the page is open in Chrome rather than inside another app. Scroll through Chrome's menu to look for “Install app” or “Add to Home screen.”
