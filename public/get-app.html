Get the App

Add When Healing Doesn't Come to your phone's Home Screen for easy access to Scripture-rooted devotionals. It's free, and no App Store download is needed.

iPhone

Use Safari to add the app.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share (the square with an arrow). Depending on your Safari layout, first tap the page menu.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Choose a name, such as When Healing, then tap Add.

If you opened the QR code inside another app, choose “Open in Safari” first.

Android

Use Chrome for the easiest setup.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm to add it to your phone.

On supported Android phones, an Install App button may appear here.
