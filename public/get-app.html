Free to install. No app store needed.

iPhone (Safari or Chrome)

You can add the app using Safari or Chrome on your iPhone.

  1. Open this page in Safari or Chrome.
  2. Tap the Share icon (a square with an arrow pointing upward). In Safari it is usually in the browser toolbar or page menu. In Chrome, look in the toolbar or browser menu.
  3. Scroll down through the Share menu. If you don’t see Add to Home Screen, tap View More (or More) to reveal additional actions, then choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. Choose a name, such as When Healing, and tap Add.

If you opened this page inside Instagram, Facebook, or another app, open it in Safari or Chrome first. If “Add to Home Screen” is missing, try Safari or check the Share menu’s available actions.

Android

Use Chrome for the easiest setup.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm to add it to your phone.

You can also use the Install App button above. If your browser cannot display an installation prompt, it will show you the steps.
