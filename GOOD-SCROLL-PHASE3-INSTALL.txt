WHDC The Good Scroll Phase 3 - Website/PWA

Requires Phase 1 and Phase 2 installed.
Replace src/good-scroll-feed.js in your website project.
Deploy with npx wrangler deploy.

Adds 1080x1350 PNG export, share image with Web Share API where supported, download fallback, and device-local saved posts.
Saved posts are stored in browser localStorage, not synced to WHDC accounts.
Native iOS app has NOT been updated. Test on your live website before preparing a native build.
